## 🆕 New Additions to the Lineup

We're thrilled to welcome two fresh pairs to MAC 2026:

### Eli & Bagaz (Acrobagaz) 🇮🇹
Matteo (aka Acrobagaz) and Elisa are bringing their unique energy all the way from North-East Italy. With 12+ years of teaching experience across Europe, a background in Acroyoga Montréal, and a style that blends tangled flows, icarians, whip-pops, and standing sequences — their classes are equal parts technical, joyful, and completely infectious.

You'll recognise them easily: he looks like a drunken rainbow, she sounds like a howler monkey (with chocolate instead of bananas). We already love them.

### Michal & Klara (Acro Klaris / Acro Misaris) 🇸🇰
Joining the Misaris family at MAC this year is Klára — a former gymnast, dance teacher, and physiotherapist — alongside Miško, the Acro Misaris founder you may already know. Together as Acro Klaris, they bring a beautiful mix of movement science, biomechanics, and flows, icarians, whip-pops, and F2F work. Expect workshops that are engaging, educational, and packed with great content.
